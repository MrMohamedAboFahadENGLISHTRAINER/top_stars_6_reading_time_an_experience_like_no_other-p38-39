Listen & Read the Story
Part 1
It was the Roberts family's first time in Egypt. They wanted to try something different, so they booked a desert tour. They set off early one morning in a special jeep. Mike was excited because the guide told him that they might ride camels later that day. An hour later, Mike began to feel bored. "Be patient, Mike," his mother said to him.
Part 2
Suddenly, they heard a strange sound and the jeep stopped. "The jeep has broken down. I'll have to fix it," said the guide. "Oh no! It's getting so hot!" complained Mike. An hour later, they were still in the desert. "Help has arrived!" said the guide.
Part 3
A group of men on camels rode up to them. They were very friendly. They greeted everyone and then worked together to put up a tent to protect everyone from the sun. They gave everyone fresh water and dates, and then they helped the guide work on the jeep. Mike looked at the tall camels. "Can I ride one?" he asked. "Wow! This is awesome!" said Mike happily.
Part 4
A while later the jeep was ready. "Thank you for all your help," said the guide to the men. "You're welcome. We are always here to help when we can," one of the men answered. "Who were those men who helped us?" "They're Bedouins. They know how to survive in the desert," the guide answered. "They were very kind and friendly. They're amazing!" said Mike.
